# Credits

Castaway Cove's code, world layout, UI and sounds are original and generated at runtime.
The visual upgrade uses the following public-domain (CC0 1.0) art packs. The full license texts
are kept in `Assets/CC0/Licenses/`, and the pack notes are in `Assets/CC0/SOURCES.md`.

Created/distributed by Kenney (kenney.nl). License: CC0 1.0.

(rock_largeA, rock_smallA, rock_smallFlatA), grass and plants (grass, grass_large, plant_bush),
cliff_rock and fence_planks.

## Quaternius — Animated Fish Pack
Created/distributed by Quaternius (quaternius.com). License: CC0 1.0.
Used for: Fish1, Fish2 and Fish3 display poses for the held-fish preview and the Giant Piranha;
Shark for the Bowhead Whale boss. The Swim clips import with the models; the held-fish display
uses a baked Swim pose so it renders reliably at hold size. The landed/attacking catch uses the
Kenney survival fish for a reliable low-poly silhouette.

## Notes
All models are imported with Unity's native FBX importer at scale 1 and standard material/texture
importing. Materials are generated by `Assets/Editor/CC0Import.cs`: Kenney packs use their
colormap atlases; Nature models and the Quaternius fish use flat colours that fit the existing
palette. Runtime placement, scale and rotation are handled in `Assets/Scripts/World/`.
